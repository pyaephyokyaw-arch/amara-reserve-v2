1Overview

The site is inquiry first. A visitor picks an experience and sends a short inquiry at no cost. Sign in or account creation is required before the inquiry is sent. A coordinator sends a quote. The customer accepts the quote and Terms, completes details and documents, and pays the package in the same step.

This IA applies the six category Experiences menu with a maximum of four items in each category, except Visa Service (9 options in 3 groups) and Leisure (5 items). Add on services (attraction tickets and day trips) are shown on the service detail page, not in the menu.

2Confirmed decisions

Confirmed 25 and 27 September and 1 October 2026, and applied on the live site. These are fixed in the sitemap and flows below.

  1. Six experience categories. Visa Service, Medical, Aesthetics, Wellness, Leisure, Transport Services. The stats bar shows 6 categories.
  2. Menu label is "Experiences". Each category shows a maximum of 4 items, except Visa Service (9 options in 3 groups) and Leisure (5 items). Add ons appear only on the service detail page.
  3. Aesthetics menu. Partner Clinics, Clinical Appointment, Facial & Body Treatments, Post-Treatment Aftercare. Partner Clinics lists each licensed clinic with its location and published rates, matching Partner Hospitals under Medical. Spa appears only under Wellness.
  4. Visa Service scope. Tourist Visa, Education Visa and Visa Extension. The menu shows the three groups with their nine visa options listed underneath. Each option opens its service detail page and shows its price or "On request". On mobile, each group opens the Visa category page filtered to that group. DTV and 90 day reporting are removed from the site.
  5. Leisure menu. Tickets & Day Trips (new, 10 items), Nature & Escape, Culture & Craft, Taste of Thailand, Movement & Mastery. Leisure has five menu items. Tickets & Day Trips also feeds the home carousel.
  6. Short inquiry form. Name, email, phone, experience type and message. No Terms step before sign in. If the visitor is not signed in, "Send inquiry" opens sign in or create account first, then the inquiry is sent automatically.
  7. Payment happens after the customer registers. Guests can browse and fill in the inquiry form, and sign in to send it. The customer accepts the quote and Terms on the details and documents page, just above Pay and submit.
  8. No fee to send an inquiry. The inquiry form has no payment. The package and government fees are paid once, on the details and documents page (3.5), after the quote is accepted.
  9. Payment methods. PromptPay, Visa, Mastercard and KBZPay. Shown in the package payment block on 3.5 and in Help Center, Fees and payment.
  10. Languages. English and Myanmar.
  11. Transport Services is a new category. Airport & Hotel Transfers, Intercity Transfers, Private Car with Driver, Car Rental.

Inquiry form fields (3.1)

Full name, email, phone or WhatsApp, experience type, anything we should know. The same five fields for guests and signed in customers. No payment, no Terms tick. Not signed in visitors sign in or create an account on submit; the form details are kept. Passport details stay out of this first form, so visitors can ask without sharing sensitive data.

Sign in and Terms (3.3)

Sign in or create account is shown on inquiry submit if the visitor is not signed in. The Terms of service and Privacy notice are accepted on the details and documents page (3.5), in a checkbox just above Pay and submit.

Package payment (part of 3.5)

Shown only to registered, signed in customers who accepted the quote, for the quoted package and government fees. No fee is paid earlier. Methods: PromptPay, Visa, Mastercard, KBZPay. Card payments go through a PCI DSS compliant payment gateway, so card numbers never touch Amara Reserve servers.

Details and documents with payment (3.5)

In order: quote summary, full name as in passport, date of birth, nationality, arrival date, passport expiry, length of stay, passport number, appointment date, passport photo page upload, anything else, package payment (PromptPay, Visa, Mastercard, KBZPay), quote and Terms checkbox, Pay and submit. No add ons on this page.

13Risks and gaps

Raise these with the CEO, Legal and the Data Protection lead before build.

  1. Passport upload and PDPA. Step 3.5 collects passport copies, date of birth and nationality. Needs a PDPA review, a privacy notice, encrypted storage, access limited to coordinators and a retention period before launch. Check with Legal or the Data Protection lead before sharing this data with partners.
  2. Terms after the inquiry. The short form has no Terms tick. Confirm with Legal that a privacy notice link on the form is enough to store inquiry contact data before the customer accepts the Terms on the details and documents page.
  3. Card payments. Visa and Mastercard need a PCI DSS compliant payment gateway. Confirm the gateway with Finance and the CTO.
  4. Agent disclosure. Visa, medical and aesthetic pages must say Amara Reserve arranges the service and is not the embassy, hospital or clinic. Add to the footer and every service detail page.
  5. Processing time promises. Show times as estimates set by the authority, not a guarantee. Add "Timing depends on the embassy or Immigration" under each price panel.
  6. Community board. User posts need moderation, report and remove tools, and board rules. Block posts that contain phone numbers or passport details.
  7. Thailand News missing. The proposal lists Thailand news, festivals and sale seasons, and a public transport guide. Proposed as Insider Notes tab 4.6, with BTS and MRT routes added to 4.3 Travel guide.
  8. Car Rental. Self drive needs a valid licence check and insurance terms. Consider launching Private Car with Driver first.
  9. Open content. Prices for Multiple-Entry Tourist Visa, Culinary & Cooking School, Hotel & Restaurant Training, both Visa Extensions, Post-Treatment Aftercare, all Transport Services items and Leisure packages (shown as "On request" or placeholders). Native review of Myanmar copy for Leisure, Visa sub menu and Transport labels.
