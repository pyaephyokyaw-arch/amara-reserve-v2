Verdict

Ready with fixes.

The flows are clear and short: a five field inquiry, hospital first on Health Packages, and payment only after a quote or slot confirmation. The biggest risk is accessibility in the light theme: bronze text on white is 3.15:1 and form borders are 1.25:1. The biggest strength is the trust model: published hospital rates, "No payment now" on every service page, and status tracking in My Inquiries.

Scope: Home, Experiences menu, Category, Service detail, Hospital Appointment, Choose Your Doctor, Booking steps, Health Packages, Partner Hospitals, Inquiry, Inquiry received, Quote and payment, Profile, Wishlist, Thailand Hub, Help, Search and Sign in, in dark and light themes. Method: source review, contrast measured from theme tokens, preview screenshots. No screen reader session.
